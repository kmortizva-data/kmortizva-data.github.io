Gold under the noise

Real geostatistics over 62,000 km² of South Australia: from 1,333 dirty records to a kriging map that confesses its own uncertainty.

  1. 01
    The brief and the terrane

    1,333 records · 79 variables · 62,000 km²

    Gold in a Proterozoic terrane. What is asked, what the file holds, and why the analysis is modular.

  2. 02
    Cleaning and integrity

    1,333 → 1,020 locations · >99% censoring in some variables

    Inconsistent coordinates, censored data, spatial redundancy. Duplicates aggregated by arithmetic mean.

  3. 03
    Gold is not normal

    log10 · bimodal · P95 = 1.57 log ≈ 37.5 ppb

    Strong skewness, the log10 transform, and a bimodality that will turn out to be geology.

  4. 04
    Gold's companions

    Au-Gd r≈+0.47 · Au-La r≈−0.34 · CoDa

    Bivariate and compositional: Gd follows gold, La walks away: hydrothermal fractionation in the data.

  5. 05
    Geology rules, statistics constrains

    15.0 ppb cut · Nxcal/Nhunt/Nhuyb · ~48 samples → global model

    The anomalies live in specific basement units, but stratifying with n<50 would wreck the variography: the global model is chosen knowing its price.

  6. 06
    The spatial signature

    Gaussian · range 4,470 m · nugget 0.105 · anisotropy 045°

    From the experimental variogram (240 m lag) to the model: maximum continuity NE-SW and mixed geometric-zonal anisotropy.

  7. 07
    Kriging: the best honest estimate

    SK and OK · RMSE 0.230 log · z up to ±6

    THE REINFORCED CHAPTER. What kriging actually is (a 3-sample system solved by hand with the project's own variogram), SK vs OK without tricks (CV cannot tell them apart), kriging variance and its heavy tails, and the moving-neighbourhood facets explained.

  8. 08
    The knobs that did not matter

    models ±0.006 RMSE · neighbours ±0.009

    Spherical 0.2371 / exponential 0.2430 / Gaussian 0.2435; n=5→0.2388 … n=40→0.2295. That the knobs barely move anything IS the finding: the real constraint is the 425 samples.

  9. 09
    Strictly preliminary

    425 of 1,333 with measurable gold · co-kriging and simulation as the way out

    Good enough to pick exploration targets, not to classify reserves. Co-kriging on Gd/La and sequential simulation as next steps, risks included.
