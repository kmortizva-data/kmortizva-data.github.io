Who I am

I am a mining and metallurgy engineer, currently on the Erasmus Mundus EMerald master in Georesources Engineering, which runs across Belgium, France and Sweden. I graduated first in my class at Universidad Nacional de Colombia, on the scholarship for best academic average.

What I want to work on is the part of the circular economy that gets skipped: recycling alone cannot feed it. A perfect loop only returns what was put in decades ago, so the metals the transition needs will still come out of ore — and out of ore that is poorer and more complex every year. That makes primary and secondary resources one problem, not two.

My angle on it is measurement. You cannot recover what you cannot characterise, and a plant cannot correct what it learns about hours late. The four projects below are how I learned to handle data at that standard. Every one closes with a measured result, including the ones that disappointed, because a number nobody can reproduce is not a number.
