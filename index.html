Who I am

I am a mining and metallurgy engineer, currently on the Erasmus Mundus EMerald master's in Georesources Engineering, which runs across Belgium, France and Sweden. I graduated first in my class at Universidad Nacional de Colombia, on the scholarship for best academic average.

The circular economy has a gap that most people skip past. Recycling can only give back metal that was already mined, decades ago, when the world used far less of it. Copper takes about forty years to come back, so even perfect recycling would cover only about half of what is needed today. The rest still has to be dug out of ore, and the ore left is poorer and more complex every year. That is why primary and secondary resources are one problem, not two.

My own angle is data analysis. Clustering thousands of papers into a map you can actually read, predicting what a plant will do in the next hour, testing whether a difference between two groups is real. The three projects below all run on real data rather than teaching sets, and each one ends with a number anybody can check.
